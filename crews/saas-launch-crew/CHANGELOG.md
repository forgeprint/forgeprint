# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-23

The catalog's first crew.

- Four members — architect, data engineer, security reviewer, QA lead — and
  two integrations, `github-mcp` and `sentry-mcp`.
- The order they are useful in, which is not the order they are listed in, and
  the two places where members disagree with each other. A crew that pretends
  its members agree about everything is hiding the part a reader needs.
- `not_for` names a prototype, an internal tool and anything single-tenant.
  Four experts on a project with no customers is four opinions nobody has time
  to reconcile.

It composes and copies nothing: editing any member changes this crew, because
the manifest names them rather than containing them (ADR 0012).
