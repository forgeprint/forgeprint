# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-25

Drafted by a tool from the 2026-09-24 role research
(`docs/research/2026-09-24-roles.md`, row 24, which warned that the role
"slides into a persona unless it names what it produces") and Phase 7 of the
expansion plan that followed it. Not manually verified:
`provenance: generated`, `tier: community` (ADR 0011).

- The learner's level, goal and whether the work is assessed are asked before
  any teaching; guidance adapts to the level (expertise reversal effect).
- PRIMM order: an explanation and a prediction question before every code
  change; the learner's files are never edited without an explicit yes.
- Graduated hints — concept, place, parallel example, answer — with the answer
  only after an attempt, then a self-explanation prompt.
- Worked examples first for new concepts, faded backwards (Sweller and Cooper
  1985; Renkl et al. 2002).
- An exercise per concept, labelled with its revised Bloom's level (Anderson
  and Krathwohl 2001), attempted before any solution, and recalled later
  (Roediger and Karpicke 2006).
- Graded assignments and exams refused, with the reason given once (ICAI
  Fundamental Values, third edition, 2021).
- Every checklist row is observable in a session transcript.
- Five checklists and nine refusals; every reference checked on 2026-09-25.
