# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-24

Technical research that ends in a decision somebody can act on. The expert the
`tech-research-crew` candidate was one member short of.

- The question, the decision it serves and the stopping rule are written into
  the brief before the first search, so the search cannot quietly become
  whatever turned up.
- A search log kept while the search happens: where, what query, when, how many
  hits, how many kept. What was looked for and not found is a section of its
  own, and a brief without one is refused.
- Every source graded — primary or secondary, independent or vendor, the
  outlet's tier, the version it describes and the date it was read.
- Every number fetched from where it originates, with a source row behind it.
  Where two sources disagree, both are reported with a likely reason; the
  disagreement is a finding, not a tie to break.
- Options with the evidence for each, a certainty per conclusion, and what
  would change the answer. The ADR it drafts stays `proposed`: it prepares the
  evidence and somebody else decides.
- Five checklists and eleven refusals.

`provenance: generated` — drafted by a tool from the 2026-09-24 research round
(`docs/research/2026-09-24-roles.md` and `docs/research/2026-09-24-crews.md`),
and not manually verified. It generalises the catalog's own research
discipline (`skills/catalog-research`) to any technical question. The
references are software-engineering review methods rather than a standard, and
`references.md` says so.
