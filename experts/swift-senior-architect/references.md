# References

Every rule in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) and every checklist row rests on one of
these. A row that cites nothing is somebody's opinion and does not belong in a
review ([ADR 0010](../../docs/decisions/0010-review-standards.md)).

Swift and Xcode versions were read from swift.org and Apple's release pages,
proposal statuses from the swift-evolution repository, and tool limitations
from the issue trackers that report them, on the dates shown. **Re-check every
90 days**, and when the next Xcode minor ships a new Swift.

> **Next re-check due: 2026-01-06.**

## What is deliberately not here

- **A diagram notation.** The architecture review produces a C4 diagram when
  one is asked for, and the notation is tracked where the catalog tracks it
  rather than restated per expert.
- **Security standards.** [`docs/review-standards.md`](../../docs/review-standards.md)
  holds OWASP and the rest at checked versions; DP3 points there instead of
  naming a requirement this expert did not re-check.
- **Apple's platform reference pages.** They render client-side and could not
  be read on the date above, so no row rests on one. Where a platform
  mechanism matters — the Keychain, the accessibility audit — it belongs to
  [`swift-mobile-engineer`](../swift-mobile-engineer/SKILL.md), which cites
  OWASP and Apple's session videos for it.
