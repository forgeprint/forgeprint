# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-25

Drafted by a tool from the 2026-09-24 role research
(`docs/research/2026-09-24-roles.md`) and Phase 7 of the expansion plan that
followed it, which scoped this expert to "methods only: ISO 9241-210,
usability testing" and, under decision D4, refused visual and UI taste. Not
manually verified: `provenance: generated`, `tier: community` (ADR 0011).

- A research question and the decision it informs before a method; the method
  justified on Rohrer's dimensions; users, goals and context of use from ISO
  9241-11:2018.
- A usability test protocol: goal-based tasks without interface clues, success
  criteria dated before the first session, all three ISO 9241-11 measures,
  neutral think-aloud prompts, a pilot.
- Sample size with its source and limits: five per formative round (Nielsen 2000) and about 40 for quantitative studies (Budiu and Moran 2021); no
  percentages from formative studies.
- SUS administered and scored as Brooke (1996) published it, compared with
  Sauro's average of 68, never described as a percentage.
- Participant consent and data minimisation from GDPR Articles 5 and 7, with
  referral for children, vulnerable participants and special categories.
- Synthesis with observations and interpretations apart (Braun and Clarke 2006) and Nielsen's 0–4 severity scale.
- Six checklists and eleven refusals; every reference checked on 2026-09-25.
