# Participant consent

Research participants lend their time, their behaviour and often their face
and voice. What is collected, and how they can take it back, is written down
before the first session.

| #   | Check                                                                                                            | How                                                   | Source                                        |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| PC1 | Consent is obtained before any recording starts                                                                  | the consent timestamp precedes the recording's        | GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), Article 7(1) |
| PC2 | The consent request is in plain language and separate from other terms                                           | read the form                                         | GDPR Article 7(2)                             |
| PC3 | Recording is a separate, optional consent                                                                        | the form has its own line for audio or video          | GDPR Article 7(2)                             |
| PC4 | The participant is told before the session how to withdraw, and withdrawing is as easy as consenting             | read the form; the method is one step                 | GDPR Article 7(3)                             |
| PC5 | Only data the research question needs is collected                                                               | each field in the data inventory maps to the question | GDPR Article 5(1)(c), data minimisation       |
| PC6 | Recordings and raw notes have a deletion date                                                                    | the data inventory's retention column                 | GDPR Article 5(1)(e), storage limitation      |
| PC7 | Reports identify participants by pseudonym only, and quotes carry no identifying detail                          | grep the report for names, employers, places          | GDPR Article 5(1)(c)                          |
| PC8 | Studies with children or vulnerable participants, or special categories of data, are referred before recruitment | the plan records the referral                         | GDPR Articles 8 and 9                         |

## Why each one

**PC1 and PC4 are the two a participant would ask about.** Recording before
consent cannot be undone, and a withdrawal route that takes an email thread is
not the one the regulation describes.

**PC5** is a design decision, not a legal one: a research question that does
not need a participant's employer should not collect it, and a report that
does not hold it cannot leak it.

**PC8** is where this expert stops. The legal basis, an impact assessment and
ethics approval belong to the privacy role and to counsel; the checklist only
makes sure the referral happens before anyone is recruited.
