# Synthesis

From sessions to findings, with the chain intact. A reader should be able to
start from any finding and reach the moments in the sessions that support it.

| #   | Check                                                                                       | How                                                                  | Source                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SY1 | Observations are logged verbatim with participant ID, task and timestamp                    | open the observation log                                             | Braun and Clarke, _Using thematic analysis in psychology_, 2006 (phase 1, familiarisation)                          |
| SY2 | Interpretations are kept in a separate layer from observations                              | two columns or two documents; no adjective in the observation column | Braun and Clarke 2006                                                                                               |
| SY3 | Every finding lists the observation IDs behind it and the number of participants showing it | read the findings table                                              | Braun and Clarke 2006 (phases 2–5, codes to themes)                                                                 |
| SY4 | Themes were reviewed against the full data set, not only the memorable sessions             | the review step is recorded                                          | Braun and Clarke 2006 (phase 4, reviewing themes)                                                                   |
| SY5 | Each usability problem has a 0–4 severity from frequency, impact and persistence            | the severity column and its three inputs                             | Nielsen, _Severity Ratings for Usability Problems_, NN/g 1994                                                       |
| SY6 | No finding is an aesthetic judgement of the design                                          | grep for "looks", "ugly", "beautiful", "modern", "clean" as findings | Expansion plan D4 (taste refused); ISO 9241-11:2018 (usability measured as effectiveness, efficiency, satisfaction) |
| SY7 | The report states which users, tasks and contexts the study did not cover                   | a limits section                                                     | ISO 9241-11:2018 (specified users, goals, context)                                                                  |

## Why each one

**SY3 is the row that separates research from opinion.** A finding with no
observations behind it is what the researcher believed before the study,
now in a report.

**SY2** makes disagreement possible. When observation and interpretation are
separate, a reader can accept what happened and still argue with what it
means; when they are merged, the reader has to take both or neither.

**SY6** keeps this expert inside the catalog's boundary. Whether a design is
attractive is not something these methods measure.
