# Research plan

What the study is for, decided before how it is run. A plan that starts from a
method answers whatever that method happens to measure.

| #   | Check                                                                                                         | How                                                                  | Source                                                                  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| RP1 | The plan states one research question and the decision it will inform                                         | read the first section; a topic ("the app") without a question fails | ISO 9241-210:2019 (human-centred design activities)                     |
| RP2 | The method is justified against the question on the attitudinal–behavioural and qualitative–quantitative axes | the plan names both positions and why                                | Rohrer, _When to Use Which User-Experience Research Methods_, NN/g 2022 |
| RP3 | A behavioural question is not answered by an attitudinal method alone                                         | a question about what people do has an observation or usage method   | Rohrer, NN/g 2022                                                       |
| RP4 | Specified users, goals and context of use are written down                                                    | three named fields in the plan                                       | ISO 9241-11:2018, definition of usability                               |
| RP5 | Recruitment criteria derive from the specified users, and screener questions do not reveal the answer         | read the screener                                                    | ISO 9241-11:2018                                                        |
| RP6 | The plan says whether the study is formative or summative                                                     | one word in the plan, and the matching sample-size source            | Rohrer, NN/g 2022 (design versus assess phases)                         |
| RP7 | The plan names what the study will not answer                                                                 | a scope-out paragraph                                                | ISO 9241-210:2019                                                       |

## Why each one

**RP1 is the whole file.** A question tied to a decision is what lets somebody
say afterwards whether the study was worth running; without it, any result
looks like a finding.

**RP3** catches the most common mismatch: asking people whether they would use
a feature and treating the answer as behaviour.

**RP4** is what makes "usable" mean something. ISO 9241-11 defines usability
only for specified users, goals and context; a plan without them has nothing
to measure against.
