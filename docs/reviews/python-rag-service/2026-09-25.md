# python-rag-service — architecture and security review

- Date: 2026-09-25
- Blueprint version: 1.0.0
- Standards: `docs/review-standards.md` as checked 2026-09-22 (next re-check due
  2026-12-21)
- Reviewer: maintainer's Claude Code agent, `/arch-review`
- Verdict: **MERGE**

## Summary

A generated blueprint, so the review is required before its pull request opens
(ADR 0011, CLAUDE.md §5c). It is the catalog's first retrieval-augmented
generation service: FastAPI, PostgreSQL 18 with pgvector 0.8.6, an HNSW index,
and a model that answers only from retrieved chunks and cites them.

It is also the first blueprint whose attack surface is its own **data**. The
LangGraph agent's risk is what a tool can be talked into doing; this one's is
what a document can talk the model into saying. The blueprint answers that the
right way round: the fence around retrieved text is treated as a mitigation,
and the controls that hold are structural — the model has no tools, an answer
citing nothing it was shown is withheld, and nobody writes to the corpus
without the operator token.

The single most important thing to know before building on it: **every caller
of `/ask` retrieves from every document.** That is declared, and it is the
first thing to change before this holds anything that not everybody may read.

The manifest claims `tier: community` and `provenance: generated`. No
`critical` or `high` findings are open.

## Findings

### 1. Every caller retrieves from every document — `medium`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:525` (step 14, `nearest`),
  `setup.md:709` (step 15, `/ask`)
- **Standard:** OWASP LLM 2025 **LLM08 Vector and Embedding Weaknesses**
  (access control over the vector store, cross-context leakage) and **LLM02
  Sensitive Information Disclosure**; API1:2023 BOLA
- **What:** `nearest` has no filter and `chunks` has no owner column, so any
  document ingested is retrievable, and quotable, by any caller of `/ask`.
- **Why it matters:** the commonest RAG leak is not an exploit, it is a
  corpus built from documents with different audiences and served to all of
  them. A reader who ingests HR and engineering documents into one instance
  has published both to everyone who can reach the port.
- **Mitigating:** declared first under "What it is NOT for" in `overview.md`,
  and `AGENTS.md` has a section on how to add a filter — including the pgvector
  specific trap, that a `WHERE` clause after an HNSW scan can return fewer than
  `k` rows unless iterative scans are enabled — and the cross-owner test to
  write.
- **Fix:** none at this scope; the blueprint has no identity to filter by. The
  documentation is what this finding asks for, and it is there.

### 2. `/ask` is unauthenticated and nothing bounds how often it is called — `medium`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:709` (step 15)
- **Standard:** OWASP LLM 2025 **LLM10 Unbounded Consumption**; API4:2023
  Unrestricted Resource Consumption
- **What:** each question is bounded — 1,000 characters, `top_k` at most 20,
  `max_answer_tokens` at most 4,000, a provider timeout, a Content-Length check
  before parsing — but anyone who reaches the port can ask as often as they
  like, and each question is two paid provider calls.
- **Why it matters:** the per-request bounds are the harder half and they are
  present. The cross-request bound is the one that turns into an invoice.
- **Mitigating:** declared under "What it is NOT for" and in `AGENTS.md` ("put
  the service behind something that authenticates callers"); Compose binds to
  loopback only.
- **Fix:** a rate limit in front of the service, keyed on an authenticated
  caller. Left to the deployment, and declared.

### 3. The fence around retrieved text is a mitigation, not a boundary — `medium`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:378` (step 12, `SYSTEM`),
  `setup.md:407` (`build_user_message`)
- **Standard:** OWASP LLM 2025 **LLM01 Prompt Injection** (indirect, via
  ingested documents)
- **What:** every chunk is wrapped in `<passage id="N">`, angle brackets in
  chunks and in the question are escaped so neither can close a fence or forge
  one, and the system instruction says passage text is never an instruction.
  Tests feed a hostile chunk and a hostile question.
- **Why it matters:** a sufficiently persuasive document can still change what
  the model writes: a false answer, a planted link, a refusal. No prompt
  construction prevents that.
- **Mitigating — and this is why it is not `high`:** the model has no tools
  and the service executes nothing it returns, so an injection cannot act; an
  answer that cites no retrieved chunk is withheld; and writing to the corpus
  needs the operator token, which closes the anonymous poisoning path
  (LLM04). The module's own docstring says the fence lowers the odds and names
  what actually holds, and `AGENTS.md` forbids adding tools without a
  checkpoint, pointing at `langgraph-agent`.
- **Fix:** none required. Keep the rule in `AGENTS.md`; it is the one a later
  change is most likely to break.

### 4. `search` claims full-text search; the blueprint delivers vector similarity — `medium`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/manifest.yaml:15`
- **Standard:** the review skill's rule that a claim wider than its
  verification is a finding, because `resolve` matches on `requirements`
- **What:** the taxonomy labels `search` "Full-text search". Retrieval here is
  cosine distance over embeddings; there is no `tsvector` and no keyword match.
- **Why it matters:** a reader who resolves on `search` expecting exact-term
  matching gets semantic retrieval, which ranks an exact product code or error
  string below a paraphrase.
- **Mitigating:** disclosed in `overview.md` under "What it is NOT for", which
  names the gap and says the tag means semantic search here.
- **Fix:** a taxonomy term for semantic or vector retrieval, in its own pull
  request; this blueprint moves to it when it exists. Recorded in the pull
  request as a missing term.

### 5. The service connects with the role that migrates — `low`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:1304` (step 27,
  `compose.yaml`)
- **Standard:** OWASP ASVS 5.0 V13 (configuration: least privilege for
  backend connections); OWASP Top 10:2025 A02
- **What:** Compose creates one superuser, used by both `alembic upgrade head`
  (which needs it for `CREATE EXTENSION`) and the running service (which needs
  `SELECT` and `INSERT`).
- **Why it matters:** local-only here, but the file is the template a reader
  copies, and a superuser connection turns any SQL-reaching bug into schema
  loss.
- **Fix:** made in this version — `AGENTS.md` now says to run migrations as
  the owner and the service as a role with `SELECT, INSERT` on the two tables
  and `USAGE` on their sequences. The Compose file stays one role because it
  is labelled local-only.

### 6. The first migration reads a constant that can change after it has run — `low`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:786` and `:814` (step 18)
- **Standard:** ADR practice (a schema decision recorded where it cannot drift);
  Twelve-Factor II
- **What:** `0001` creates `vector(EMBEDDING_DIMENSIONS)` from the application
  constant, so the task's one-named-setting requirement is met — and a fresh
  database built after somebody edits the constant gets a different schema
  from an old one.
- **Mitigating:** `test_the_column_is_as_wide_as_the_vectors_the_code_makes`
  compares the live column to the constant and fails on drift, the OpenAI
  adapter refuses vectors of the wrong width, and `AGENTS.md` says changing
  the number is a new migration plus a re-embed and that `0001` is never
  edited.
- **Fix:** none now. When the first width change happens, the new migration
  should carry its own literal.

### 7. The base image is pinned by tag, not by digest — `low`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:1351` and `:1356` (step 33)
- **Standard:** CIS Docker Benchmark v1.8.0, image and Dockerfile scope
  (trusted, pinned base images); SLSA v1.2; OWASP Top 10:2025 A03
- **What:** `python:3.13.15-slim-trixie` is a patch-level tag, the same
  practice as the rest of the catalog, and tags can be re-pushed.
- **Fix:** pin `@sha256:` when the image is promoted beyond local use. Not
  applied here, to stay consistent with the catalog until it decides.

### 8. `answer` is model output and may contain planted markup — `info`

- **Where:** `blueprints/python-rag-service/setup.md:709` (step 15)
- **Standard:** OWASP LLM 2025 **LLM05 Improper Output Handling**
- **What:** the API returns the model's text as a JSON string and renders
  nothing, so there is no sink in the blueprint itself.
- **Fix:** made in this version as documentation — `AGENTS.md` says a client
  renders `answer` as text, never as HTML.

## What was checked and found sound

- **Grounding is enforced, not requested.** `cited_ids` keeps only ids that
  were retrieved; an answer with none is returned as `not_grounded` with no
  text; an empty store returns `nothing_to_answer_from` and a test counts that
  the model was not called (LLM09 Misinformation).
- **The fake is never the silent default.** `MODEL_PROVIDER` defaults to the
  real provider, which refuses to start without `OPENAI_API_KEY` and
  `OPENAI_CHAT_MODEL`, naming them; a setup step starts the container without a
  key and reads the refusal. Every answer carries `provider`, so fake output
  cannot pass for real (A10; Twelve-Factor III).
- **Secrets.** `SecretStr` for the key and the token, nothing secret-shaped in
  any file, `local-development-only-not-a-real-secret` and
  `ci-only-not-a-real-secret` as placeholders, `.env` git-ignored and
  docker-ignored (CLAUDE.md §5b).
- **The ingest token** is compared with `hmac.compare_digest`, required to be
  at least 32 characters, and tested for missing and wrong values (ASVS 5.0
  V6 authentication, for an operator credential).
- **Provider failures** are a 502 with a generic message; the cause is logged
  by type only, and a test proves nothing was stored because embedding happens
  before the first insert (A10; LLM02).
- **The provider is asked not to store requests** (`store=False`), and a test
  checks the adapter sends it, with the answer-token bound (LLM02; LLM10).
- **Input bounds appear in the published contract.** The field limits are in
  `/openapi.json` and a test asserts them, so `api-docs` documents what is
  enforced. A POST without Content-Length gets 411 and one over 1 MiB gets
  413 before any parsing (API4).
- **The chunking rule is stated once and tested word by word**, including the
  cut on words over 32 characters that keeps a chunk under an embedding
  model's input limit (LLM10).
- **The index choice is justified in the migration.** HNSW because IVFFlat
  built on an empty table has poor recall until rebuilt; cosine operators to
  match the query, and `nearest` keeps the `ORDER BY … LIMIT` shape the index
  serves.
- **Retrieval order is tested against real pgvector**, not a mock: three
  documents, a question sharing words with one, that one first and strictly
  closer.
- **One file names a provider**; routes hold no SQL; `/health` touches neither
  the database nor the model (hexagonal ports and adapters).
- **Container:** multi-stage, non-root UID 10001, patch-pinned base, no
  secrets in layers, tests and local files excluded by `.dockerignore`; the
  container does not migrate on start (CIS Docker, image scope).
- **Compose** publishes both ports on loopback, on ports the operating system
  chooses.
- **CI** pins both actions by commit SHA, has `permissions: contents: read`,
  installs from the same requirements file the recipe does, and needs no
  secret (SSDF PS.1, PW.4; SLSA v1.2).
- **Every version pinned** with `==` and read live from PyPI and Docker Hub on
  2026-09-25 (A03).

## Not applicable

- **User authentication and session management (ASVS 5.0 V6, V7).** There are
  no users; the one operator token is covered above. `overview.md` says so and
  points at `fastapi-service`.
- **Multi-tenancy.** Not claimed; finding 1 is the related data-exposure point.
- **Excessive agency (LLM06) and the Agentic 2026 list.** No tools, no loop,
  no actions — the model returns text only. Recorded as the reason finding 3
  is `medium`.
- **MCP specification and the OWASP MCP guide.** No MCP surface.
- **Transport security.** The service speaks plain HTTP on loopback in Compose;
  TLS terminates in front of it in any deployment, which is the reader's
  environment.
- **System prompt leakage (LLM07).** The system prompt holds no secret; its
  text is in the repository.

---

## Verdict

**MERGE.** Four `medium`, three `low`, one `info`; no `critical` or `high`.

Two of the `medium` findings (1, 2) are the same shape: a control that belongs
to an identity this blueprint does not have, declared where a reader looks.
Finding 3 is the honest limit of prompt construction, and the blueprint says
so itself in the code. Finding 4 is a taxonomy gap, disclosed. Findings 5 and
8 were closed in this version by rules in `AGENTS.md`.

`tier: official` is not available while `provenance: generated` (ADR 0011).
